# Matched-budget mean / CVaR / best-of-K comparison

Frozen before execution. Offline only; no accounts, hardware jobs, paper edits,
or changes to earlier Q.NILM experiments. This is the six-qubit synthetic
follow-up to the exploratory objective screen, not held-out NILM validation.

## Primary comparison

Fit p=1 on the same 680 W example: load levels [0,400,900] and [0,120,280] W.
Keep W initialization, ordered chain-XY mixer and categorical cost unchanged.
Compare five losses: mean, lower CVaR at alpha=0.25 and 0.50, expected best-of-4
and expected best-of-16. Normalize all losses by the same coefficient scale.
No known optimum or appliance truth is used for loss selection or fitting.

Use five paired trials, with two restart seeds each:
(9101,9102), (9201,9202), (9301,9302), (9401,9402), (9501,9502).
The first pair deliberately reproduces the earlier mean fit; this is not a
blind new seed study. Each restart receives exactly 512 evaluations: 32 initial
candidates (uniform plus 31 seeded random), then bounded Powell from the two
best distinct candidates, each allotted 240 calls. Unused local-search calls
are filled with seeded random candidates. All calls, including repetitions,
count. The same seed produces the same initial pool for every loss. Which
initial points seed Powell, and subsequent adaptive points, may differ by loss.
Ties select the first evaluated candidate; no exact-mean secondary reranking.

Three prespecified configurations give 75 fits / 76,800 training evaluations:

| Configuration | Measurement model during training | Gamma bound | Fits |
|---|---|---|---:|
| exact8 | Exact ideal distribution | [0,8π] | 25 |
| sampled8 | 256 ideal iid measurement shots per call | [0,8π] | 25 |
| exact16 | Exact ideal distribution | [0,16π] | 25 |

Beta is always in [0,π]. The wider-bound arm tests the earlier boundary finding,
not a post-result expansion. The sampled arm uses the same Powell policy; this
is a finite-shot sensitivity test, not a claim Powell is the best noisy optimizer.
Independent RNG streams for measurements and candidate generation prevent
shot sampling from altering the initial pools. Measurement seed = restart seed
+ 1,000,000. Every call draws 256 uniforms and maps them through its candidate
distribution. Common random numbers pair call indices across losses, not
necessarily identical angles. The sample loss is the mean, lowest 64/128 costs,
or mean of 64 groups of four / 16 groups of sixteen minima, respectively.
The minimum observed training loss chooses the candidate; exact evaluation
after training is reporting only. Total sampled training shots: 6,553,600,
all generated classically, not QPU shots. Save complete evaluation traces.

## Evaluation and scope

Evaluate every frozen fit exactly on the training aggregate and on the other
eight aggregates generated by all state pairs from the same two load models.
These eight are synthetic transfer checks, not fresh real-data test windows.
Do not select a winning trial, loss, bounds or parameters using transfer truth.
Report all five trial values and mean/range, with equal weight per trial; range
is optimizer-seed variation, not a confidence interval. No significance claim.
Include the uniform feasible distribution and exact enumeration as controls.

For raw budgets 4,16,256, report probability of at least one optimum and
expected decoded objective / appliance MAE. Compare against the actual
minimum-sampled-cost decoder (ties lower feasible index), not the most likely
state. Appliance truth is evaluation-only. Do not confuse synthetic expected
MAE with the paper's held-out MAE. Large-budget saturation is reported.

## Fixed noise evaluation, without noisy angle tuning

Only the 25 exact8 fits plus the W-only uniform circuit receive physical-model
noise evaluation on the primary 680 W example. This deliberately separates
finite-shot training sensitivity from hardware-model transfer; sampled8 and
exact16 have no device-noise results in this campaign.

Use the existing six-qubit diagnostic compiler, fixed Fez layouts 128–133 and
140–145, seed 17, optimization level 1, and the archived 14 September 2026
16:30:07 UTC calibration. Check every compiled ideal against the logical
distribution. Evaluate the combined calibration approximation on both layouts
(52 density simulations), and the common generic model on layout A (26):
78 density simulations total. Reuse the established gate/idle and readout
models unchanged. No crosstalk, leakage, drift or actual pulse schedule is
claimed. W-only is a physical preparation control, not the classical feasible
uniform sampler; report their differing resources and noise exposure honestly.

Report raw valid probability and raw-budget optimal-hit probability; all-invalid
batches are explicit abstentions. Conditional best cost and MAE are accompanied
by no-valid probability. No repair, postselection-only headline, fabricated
fallback value or replacement of the earlier IBM MAE is permitted.
